# Privacy policy

Shorts Generator is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

It accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, to upload videos and read the channel's own statistics.

It does not collect, store or share data about any other person. It does not sell data or share data with third parties. Login tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
